## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Subtractor, Mälstrom, and Thor** — Three versatile virtual synthesizers that provide a range of sounds from classic Moog emulations to unique 8-bit creations.
- **Redrum** — A powerful rhythm box equipped with 10 sample cells, offering extensive controls for editing tone and length of samples.
- **NN-19, NN-XT, and Dr. REX** — Emulates hardware samplers, allowing for flexible sampling and playback of various sounds and phrases.
- **Rack of Effects** — A comprehensive collection of effects including reverb, delay, vocoder, filters, and equalizers to enhance your sound.
- **MClass Mastering Suite** — A set of dynamic processors to ensure a professional finish to your music productions.

### Advanced Features:
- **ReWire Compatibility** — Allows communication with other applications for expanded functionality.
- **MIDI Controller Support** — Seamlessly integrates with MIDI keyboards for enhanced control.
- **ASIO Sound Card Compatibility** — Ensures low-latency audio performance.
